Our story

We source every ball ourselves — that's why they're cheaper.

Every ball we sell is sourced second-hand through arrangements with West Midlands golf courses and deals with local golfers — never bought in from a wholesaler, and never taken without permission. Because we source everything ourselves, there's no middleman mark-up, which is the real reason our prices sit well under the rest of the market.

It also means fewer new balls manufactured, and fewer left behind on a course for good. We're a small, local, hands-on operation — not a warehouse — and every single ball is checked by hand before it goes in a bundle.
