Used Titleist Golf Balls

The UK's most-played ball, collected second-hand and re-graded — Pro V1, Velocity, NXT Tour and mixed models, at a fraction of the price of new.

Hand-checked Free UK shipping West Midlands

Titleist is the name most golfers reach for first — and our second-hand Titleist golf balls let you play it without the new-ball price tag. Sourced locally through arrangements with West Midlands courses and golfers, every ball is hand-checked, cleaned and graded before it goes in a box. Choose Pro V1 for the UK's most-played tour ball, Velocity or NXT Tour for a distance-focused game, or a Titleist Mixed pack for the best value per ball.

Every listing is graded A+ or B — A+ covers anything from a light Grade A up to Mint, and B is a solid second-hand ball for the range or a casual round. See the grading FAQ for the full detail.
