Used Srixon Golf Balls

Consistent spin, a soft response, and a price that makes sense: AD333, Distance and Soft Feel.

Hand-checked Free UK shipping West Midlands

Srixon golf balls are known among UK golfers for consistent spin and a soft, responsive feel, and our second-hand Srixon balls deliver the same performance for less. Every ball is sourced locally from outside West Midlands courses and through deals with local golfers, hand-checked, cleaned and graded by condition. Choose AD333 for the range-leading all-rounder, Soft Feel for a gentler compression, or Distance for the budget-friendly option.

Every listing is graded A+ or B. A+ covers anything from a light Grade A up to Mint, and B is a solid second-hand ball for the range or a casual round. See the grading FAQ for the full detail.
