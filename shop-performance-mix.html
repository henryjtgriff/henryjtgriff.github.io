Performance Mix

TaylorMade, Bridgestone, Wilson Staff, Maxfli and Nike in one bundle — the top-brand mix, graded A+ or B.

Hand-checked Free UK shipping West Midlands

Performance Mix is our top-brand bundle — TaylorMade, Bridgestone, Wilson Staff, Maxfli and Nike, all sourced second-hand through arrangements with West Midlands courses and golfers, hand-checked and cleaned before they go in the box. It's the pick for golfers who want recognisable names without paying full price for a single-brand sleeve.

Every listing is graded A+ or B — A+ covers anything from a light Grade A up to Mint, and B is a solid second-hand ball for the range or a casual round. See the grading FAQ for the full detail.
